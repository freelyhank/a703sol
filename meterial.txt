PRODUCT
Konvoxa is an independent Solana community application with a consensus-workspace
interface. It supports overview, quests, token status, advisory governance,
treasury reads, and a participation guide. Routes remain #home / #quests /
#stake / #governance / #treasury / #learn.

The Anchor community program records quest participation and advisory votes.
It does not mint or distribute tokens, implement token locks, or execute
treasury spending. No token mint or token symbol is configured.
